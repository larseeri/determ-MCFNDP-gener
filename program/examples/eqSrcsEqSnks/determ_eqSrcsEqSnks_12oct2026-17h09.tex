\section{Notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item{Black arcs are part of the base, grid secondary topology.}
\item{Red arcs are additional, randomly generated.}
\item{$C=x$: overall capacity of arc is equal to $x$.}
\item{$F=y$: fixed cost of arc is equal to $y$.}
\item{See below for capacities and variable costs per arc, per commodity.}
\item{Circular nodes are transit nodes.}
\item{Rectangular nodes are source ($flow > 0$) or sink ($flow < 0$) nodes for stated commodities.}
\end{itemize}
